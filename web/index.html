Four delays in every ping

  • Propagation Distance. Fixed by physics.
  • Serialization Putting bits on the wire. Tiny.
  • Processing Routers handling packets. Small.
  • Queuing Waiting in line. Grows when busy.

L4 · TCP

Your plan is bandwidth; this is throughput, what actually arrives. Loss and long round trips make TCP slow down.

  • Retest on a cable. Fine wired? The limit is Wi-Fi.
  • Fix loss or bufferbloat first; speed often follows.

L3 · ICMP

An ICMP echo request and reply (a TCP handshake if ICMP is blocked). With nothing queued, it's your baseline delay.

Under 20 ms20–50 msOver 50 ms
  • Far above the physics floor? Check the route.
  • Hop 1 slow? The problem is inside your home.

Oversized queues make calls and games wait behind downloads. QoS queuing keeps urgent packets moving.

Delay variation

The changing part of delay, usually queuing or Wi-Fi retries. Too much and calls break up.

Under 10 ms10–20 msOver 20 ms
  • Wi-Fi only? Use 5 GHz or move closer.
  • Only under load? Fix bufferbloat.

Packet lossPackets that never arrive.
L1–2 · L3 · L4

Caused by link errors (bad signal or cable) or full router queues. TCP resends what's lost, which costs speed.

Under 1%1–5%Over 5%
  • Loss when idle: check Wi-Fi, cables, modem.
  • Loss only under load: full queues, so fix bufferbloat.
  • Find the hop: pathping speed.cloudflare.com
Cloudflare · Packet loss Microsoft · pathping
RouteEach router on the way, and where delay is added.
L3 · TTL

Each router lowers a packet's TTL by one. At zero it replies "time exceeded", revealing itself. That's traceroute.

  • Hop 1 is your router. It should be a few ms.
  • "No answer" is a router rate-limiting replies, not a fault.
Cloudflare · ICMP and traceroute Microsoft · tracert
Physics floorThe fastest a ping could ever be.
Propagation delay

Light in fibre covers about 100 km per 1 ms of round trip. The gap above the floor is what you can improve.

  • Near the floor: nothing to gain.
  • Below it: your IP location is approximate.
Cloudflare · Distance and latency Cisco · Propagation delay
Where the ratings come fromEvery threshold is published by the source named.
  • Ping, jitter, loss, bufferbloat, gaming: Cloudflare's AIM bands. Gaming takes the weakest of loss, ping and bufferbloat. AIM leaves ping between 50 and 100 ms unscored, so it counts as poor here.
  • Video calls: Microsoft's Teams targets. Under 100 ms round trip, 30 ms jitter, 1% loss.
  • Streaming: Netflix. 15 Mbps for 4K, 5 for 1080p, 3 for 720p.

Fair testingMake results comparable.
  • Test wired and on Wi-Fi; the difference is your home network.
  • Pause other downloads and streams.
  • Test at different times; evening dips mean ISP congestion.
